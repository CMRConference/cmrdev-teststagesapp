Paper submission details

Instructions for authors

  1. 1. Submission platform

    All papers must be submitted through Microsoft CMT.

    • 1.1Add every author to the CMT system while submitting the paper.
    • 1.2Do not put author details in the PDF; enter all author information only in the CMT portal.
    • 1.3New authors cannot be added once the paper has been submitted for review.
    • 1.4Removing an author is possible only with proper justification; the Organizing Committee takes the final decision.

  2. 2. Double-blind review

    ICICIC 2027 follows a double-blind peer-review process.

    • 2.1Papers are evaluated on quality, presentation and relevance; authors and reviewers stay anonymous to each other.
    • 2.2Papers that do not respect the double-blind rules are rejected without review.
    • 2.3Remove author names and affiliations from the title page of the review PDF (add them only in the camera-ready copy).
    • 2.4Remove acknowledgements, funding sources, project titles or system names that could reveal who the authors are.
    • 2.5Cite your own previous work in the third person, e.g. "the authors in [9] proposed…" instead of "we proposed…".

  3. 3. Manuscript format

    • 3.1Use the standard IEEE two-column conference template.
    • 3.2Follow the page limit given in the call for papers.

  4. 4. Camera-ready checks

    • 4.1Camera-ready copies of accepted papers go through a similarity (plagiarism) check.
    • 4.2Papers that fail the checks are rejected.

  5. 5. Presentation requirement

    Presentation mode: offline (in person at CMR University, Bengaluru).

    • 5.1At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present the paper in person.
    • 5.2The Organizing Committee may exclude papers that are not presented from the post-conference proceedings.

  6. 6. Originality, plagiarism and double submission

    • 6.1Papers must be original, unpublished and free of plagiarised material.
    • 6.2Papers must not be under review or submitted elsewhere at the same time.
    • 6.3Authors of accepted papers must sign the copyright form and present the paper at the conference.

Submission checklist

  • All papers should be original, unpublished, and not submitted to any other journals or conferences.
  • All submitted papers will be checked for plagiarism; papers not satisfying the criteria will be rejected.
  • Initial submissions should follow the provided template. Camera-ready files may be requested in Microsoft Word format.
  • Notification of acceptance will be communicated through email.
  • Registration is confirmed after payment of the fee, and at least one author must be present at the conference.

Review process

  1. Initial compliance check (scope, formatting, and originality)

  2. Double-blind peer review by domain experts

  3. Revision and camera-ready submission

  4. Final confirmation after registration and presentation commitment

Selected papers will be included in conference proceedings with ISBN numbers. Extended versions may be considered for journal publication subject to editorial policies and additional charges.

Ethics & compliance

  • Double submission is not allowed. Manuscripts under review elsewhere will be rejected.
  • Authors must cite prior work accurately and disclose conflicts of interest.
  • Camera-ready papers must incorporate reviewer feedback and formatting checks.

Copyright & consent

Authors confirm copyright and publication consent during camera-ready submission.

No-show policy

Papers not presented at the conference may be excluded from the final proceedings.
