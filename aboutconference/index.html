A global forum for intelligent computing research

The International Conference on Intelligent Computing, Information and Communication Technologies (ICICIC 2027) is a premier international forum dedicated to research excellence, innovation, and knowledge exchange across emerging areas of computing and communication technologies.

Oct 8-9, 2027 CMR University, Bengaluru, India Offline (in person)

Our vision

  • Advance responsible computing research and interdisciplinary collaboration.
  • Bridge academic innovation with real-world industry and societal challenges.
  • Provide a high-trust platform for early-career researchers and doctoral scholars.

Objectives

  • Showcase peer-reviewed research and impactful applications.
  • Facilitate cross-disciplinary exchange among computing, data, and communication domains.
  • Enable mentorship, networking, and collaborative opportunities across academia and industry.
Scope

Rigorous theory, real-world deployment

Scope & impact

ICICIC covers intelligent computing, information systems, communication technologies, and the applied research that drives them. The conference encourages papers that combine rigorous theory with real-world deployment in healthcare, education, mobility, sustainability, and enterprise systems.

ICICIC connects researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students through rigorous peer review, high-impact technical sessions, and collaborative workshops focused on intelligent systems and responsible technology adoption.
