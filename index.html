About

Know About ICICIC

Bringing researchers, academicians and industry together to advance intelligent computing.

The International Conference on Intelligent Computing, Information and Communication Technologies (ICICIC 2027) brings together researchers, academicians, industry professionals and students to share original research and real-world innovation in intelligent computing, information systems and communication technologies, under the theme Computational Intelligence for Smart Systems.

Theme of the conference

Computational Intelligence for Smart Systems

Aim of the conference

  • Provide an international forum to present original, peer-reviewed research in computational intelligence and smart systems.
  • Connect academia, industry and government to turn research into practical, sustainable technology.
  • Encourage interdisciplinary work across computing, communication, energy, control and sensing.
  • Support early-career researchers and doctoral scholars through mentorship, feedback and publication.
  • Promote responsible and ethical use of AI and emerging technologies in society.

About the School

School of Science and Computer Studies (SSCS)

The School of Science and Computer Studies (SSCS) at CMR University is dedicated to advancing technology-driven education and research. With specialised programmes in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing and Game Development, the School blends academic rigour with practical exposure to nurture future-ready professionals.

Our history

Over the years, SSCS has forged partnerships with leading technology firms, contributed to impactful faculty and student research, and facilitated placements in reputed organisations.

The School hosts technical events, hackathons, gamethons and exhibitions, giving students platforms to demonstrate their expertise beyond the classroom.

Vision

To provide high-quality education that develops globally competent technocrats and entrepreneurs in Computer Science and Technology, grounded in ethical values and equipped to meet the dynamic demands of industry and society.

Mission

  • Deliver value-based technical education in Computer Applications and Information Technology using modern infrastructure and pedagogical innovation.
  • Develop ethical and ambitious professionals through a blend of theory and practical application.
  • Build independent and collaborative problem-solving skills.
  • Create globally aware professionals who promote sustainable, environmentally conscious computing.
