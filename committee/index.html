Chief Patron

Dr. Sabitha Ramamurthy

Chancellor · CMR University

Dr. Sabitha Ramamurthy is a visionary academic leader and the Chancellor of CMR University. She chose to carry forward the dream of her father-in-law, Sri. Chikka Muniyappa Reddy, whose vision was to educate the masses. When the CMR Jnanadhara Trust was started in 1990, she assumed its leadership as President and began strengthening its educational mission. Under her guidance, the Trust established the National Junior School, now known as CMR National Public School. What began as a modest school in a family-owned orchard has since developed into a full-fledged CBSE institution known for its steady academic accomplishments. Her work did not stop with one institution. Through sustained leadership, long-term vision, and a deep commitment to quality education, Dr. Sabitha Ramamurthy has helped shape the growth of the CMR educational ecosystem and continues to inspire academic excellence, institutional development, and holistic learning.

Prof. H. B. Raghavendra

Vice Chancellor · CMR University

Prof. H. B. Raghavendra is the Vice Chancellor of CMR University, Bengaluru. Before joining CMR University, he served in major academic leadership roles including Director and Director General (Vice Chancellor) at Pandit Deendayal Petroleum University, and later as Vice Chancellor at The NorthCap University and Chandigarh University. He brings more than 35 years of experience in teaching, research, and academic governance. His distinguished leadership profile reflects sustained contributions to higher education administration and institutional growth. At CMR University, he supports academic excellence, research culture, governance quality, and the advancement of a dynamic and future-oriented university environment.

Dr. Praveen R serves as Pro-Vice Chancellor of CMR University and oversees key academic and administrative functions of the institution. His responsibilities include policy formulation, regulatory compliance, and the implementation of institutional systems and processes. He is recognised as a committed academician who combines administrative leadership with scholarly engagement and active participation across academic communities. His role contributes significantly to strengthening institutional effectiveness, academic coordination, and the overall quality framework that supports teaching, research, and university operations.

SSCS Patron

Dr. Ashok Kumar Thavani Andu

Director, School of Science and Computer Studies · CMR University

Dr. Ashok Kumar Thavani Andu is an Educationist and Academician by profession. He has a doctorate degree in Computer Applications and Master of Philosophy in Computer Science. Also, he has completed his Post Graduation in Computer Applications and Human Resource Management from Bharathiar University, Coimbatore. His command over trends in Computer Technology and Management and deep insight of students psyche and needs gives advantage to the organization. His global stint with Software Development and Management Consulting gives leverage to the organization in the art of Management. He is a people person in the institution and excels in Innovative Management, People, Process and Project Management. He has devised many new techniques and tools for effective learning, assessment and review and accelerated performance. He supports and coordinates with other departments like Computer Science, IT, Management and Operations for Proactive Customer Service. Dean by position, he prefers to be viewed as a committed resource of the organization. He believes in TEAM work and has developed a team of reliable professionals committed to the vision of the organization and inculcated in them the spirit of Generative Thinking, Creativity, Positive Approach and Mind Mapping.
